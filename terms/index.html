4. Captures and gameplay

Live observations only

A reward-eligible capture must be a short live video recorded inside Terra Fauna. Imported photos, prerecorded video, photographs of screens or printed images, toys, taxidermy, staged subjects, and manipulated media are not valid wildlife captures.

Verification

A capture may be accepted only if the Service can verify a supported species at sufficient confidence, obtain a fresh qualifying location sample, confirm that the location is eligible, and determine that the same account has not already captured the same species within the applicable duplicate radius. A user guess may contribute only as described in the game rules and cannot override weak visual evidence.

Restricted locations

Captures are unavailable in or near reviewed zoos, aquariums, safari or wildlife parks, petting zoos, and similar captive-animal facilities. Boundary data can be incomplete or change over time. There is no right to a reward because an animal or location appears eligible, and a boundary decision does not authorize entry onto any property.

Teams, stats, and progression

Earth, Air, Fire, and Water team choices are permanent under the first-release rules. Deferring a choice may reduce XP until you select a team. Species rarity, quality bands, animal stats, XP, food, duplicate distance, and other rules are game mechanics. We may balance or version these systems prospectively. Previously awarded values may remain tied to the rules in effect when a capture was accepted.

Classification is fallible

Animal identification is automated and can be wrong. Terra Fauna is a game, not a scientific survey, veterinary service, wildlife-management authority, safety tool, or emergency service. Never touch, handle, eat, approach, or make a safety decision about an animal based on a Terra Fauna result.
